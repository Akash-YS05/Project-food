# App Icon + Splash Ideas

## Visual direction

- Premium bakery mood with clean white base, fresh green trust cues, and warm cream highlights
- Green veg symbol used consistently across both apps
- Rounded premium shapes instead of generic flat ecommerce visuals

## Customer app icon

- Cream circular background
- Centered green square Pure Veg symbol
- Small cake silhouette with warm golden frosting accent
- `BB` monogram subtly embossed below the cake mark

## Admin app icon

- Deeper green background with white bakery storefront outline
- Pure Veg symbol in one corner
- Minimal clipboard or dashboard grid detail to suggest operations

## Splash ideas

### Customer app

- Full-screen soft cream background
- Center logo lockup: cake + veg symbol + `Bam Bam Cake Shop`
- Tagline below: `100% Pure Veg Bakery & Fast Food`
- Bottom caption: `Freshly baked. Purely vegetarian.`

### Admin app

- Deep green to warm olive gradient
- Storefront illustration with moving order dots or checklist motif
- Caption: `Live orders, live inventory, pure veg control`
